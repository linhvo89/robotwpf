Continuous trajectory motion type control
commands:
Startingwith HR version 6.3.3,the trajectory functionality has been optimized and
integrated. Somebasic conceptsare as follows:
Thecoordinate type oftheoriginal point dataofa trajectory: there are two types: joint
coordinates (0)and spatial coordinates (1). When acertain trajectory iscreated, the
coordinate type ofits original pointdata must bedetermined. Once atrajectory is
created, this attributecannot be changed.
MovePathJ and MovePathL: Atrajectory canberun in eitherMovePathJ or
MovePathL mode. The premiseis that thecorresponding target pointcan be
calculated.
Calculation oftarget pointposition ofatrajectory: after theoriginal pointposition
datais collected, thesystem will automatically start thecalculation oftarget point
position,becausethere are two kinds ofmovement methods, so there are two groups
oftarget pointposition calculation, and theresults ofthetwo groups of calculation are
saved in thedatabase respectively.
Import and export oftrajectory: In the front-end interface, you can exportthe
trajectory to afileor importthetrajectory fileintothe system. 6.3.3version only
supports theimportand exportof thetrajectory with thejointcoordinates as the
original pointposition.
Thestatus oftrajectory: there are 8kinds ofstatus values: not teaching (0), teaching
(1), teaching finished (4), calculating (2), calculation error (5), calculation completed
(3), imported (9), post-import processing (10), among which, teaching finished,
calculating, imported, post-importprocessing are temporary statuses, thesystem will
automatically process them andswitch them to theother statuses; imported and
